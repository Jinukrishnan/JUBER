People concept: cartoon vector style

Flat bold colours and thick dark outlines, like the vehicles. Each outfit the game dresses a person in, seen from the front and the back: saris with a contrasting blouse and a gold border, churidhars, mundus, school uniforms and everyday clothes. The 3D people in the game follow the same outfits and colours.
