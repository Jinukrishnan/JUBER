# Other Traffic Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (the user chose inline "native" execution) to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Cars, autos, bikes and buses drive the loop, keep left, queue, obey the signals and the turn rules; hitting one is an accident, stops both, and its driver shouts a Malayalam line.

**Architecture:** Pure, tested simulation in `src/game/logic/traffic.ts` (agents on the road network, car-following, signals, turns, contact) and `trafficTalk.ts` (the lines). `Traffic.tsx` steps it each frame from the player's pose, draws nearby agents with the existing cartoon models (new `paint` prop, new bus), and turns a contact into the store's accident, a speech bubble and a voice line. Work is done on branch `feat/traffic` (from `feat/annawadi`) and merged when green.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 (see `AGENTS.md`), React 19, TypeScript, R3F v9, Zustand, Vitest 5.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-traffic-design.md` (builds on the loop-map spec).

## Global Constraints

- Coordinates in metres, +x east, +z south, heading 0 faces +x, traffic keeps left. Lane centre is **3.7 m** left of the road centreline (`LANE = 3.7`).
- Cruise speeds: bike 9, car 8.5, auto 6.5, bus 7 m/s (per-vehicle variation 0.85 to 1.1). Vehicle sizes (length x width m): bike 2.1 x 0.8, car 4.4 x 1.8, auto 2.7 x 1.4, bus 10 x 2.6. Minimum gap 6 m plus 0.8 s of speed.
- Density: 6 cars, 3 autos, 3 bikes, 2 buses (14); none spawn within 40 m of the depot spot.
- Hit: closing speed above 1.5 m/s (`MIN_HIT_SPEED`), once per agent per contact, cooldown 8 s, agent stops 4 s, `registerAccident()` (Rs. 300, star off). Player speed times 0.3 as for a person.
- Seeded `mulberry32` everywhere; no `Math.random` in logic or setup. Agents never make a turn `isWrongTurn` forbids; they never leave `drivable`.
- No overtaking, lane changes, honking, engine sounds for other vehicles, or yielding inside junction boxes (agents are not hit-tested against each other).
- Malayalam wording is invented: keep it in `trafficTalk.ts` only and flag it for a native speaker.
- CRLF files: edit with `.replace(/\r\n/g, "\n")` scripts or the Edit/Write tools; no large heredocs with apostrophes in the shell (use the Write tool).
- Commits use `-c user.name="Jinu" -c user.email="sarath@zilmoney.com"` and end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

- Two agents never overlap in a lane after a crowded start, including at a junction exit (the follower sees the leader on the exit road) and queued behind a stopped, hit agent.
- A yellow light that turns while an agent is too close to stop: it goes through rather than slamming to a halt; a red-light queue releases in order on green; no agent is left stopped forever (gridlock, a stopped agent in the box).
- An agent standing in the player's way or the player standing in an agent's way: the agent follows, never passes through; a player stopped at the stop line is a leader.
- A hit while the player reverses into an agent, a hit at a junction, two agents hit in the same frame, a hit during pause or after the day ends: one accident each, no stale bubble or speech afterwards, no accident from a stopped player (below 1.5 m/s).
- Day restart or going back to the menu leaves no agents, bubbles or timers from the old day.
